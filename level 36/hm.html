Animals

Lion

The lion is a large wild animal that lives mainly in Africa. Lions live in groups called prides. They are powerful animals and are known for their strength and hunting abilities.

Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals. They are known for their large ears, long trunks and strong memory. Elephants usually live in groups and eat plants.

Tiger

The tiger is a large wild cat with orange fur and black stripes. Tigers are excellent hunters and can live in different habitats. They usually live alone.

Giraffe

The giraffe is famous for its very long neck and legs. It is the tallest land animal. Giraffes mainly eat leaves from trees and live in groups.

Panda

The giant panda is a bear that is easily recognized by its black and white fur. Pandas mainly eat bamboo and spend much of their time eating and resting.
